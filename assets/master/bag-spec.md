# The Bag Sheet — canonical spec for "the same bag" (blueprint §8, verbatim intent)

Every generation prompt embeds this sheet. It is the single source of truth for
what "the same bag" means across every asset.

- 340g (12oz) flat-bottom stand-up pouch, visible side gussets, standing upright.
- Body: matte charcoal-kraft laminate, colour `#2B2724`. **Matte** — no gloss, no
  plastic sheen, no reflected softbox.
- One horizontal ember-orange band (`#E2662B`), occupying the lower third,
  unbroken, letterpress-debossed.
- Wordmark **EMBER & OAK** centred on the band in condensed letterspaced
  capitals, warm off-white, one line, correctly spelled.
- A single oak-leaf-and-flame mark, ~26mm, centred above the wordmark, one
  consistent line weight, warm off-white.
- One 20mm matte-black one-way degassing valve, upper-right quadrant of the face.
- Roll-top closure folded twice, creased, with a copper tin-tie visible.
- Counter: dark oak, open grain, matte, no varnish highlights.
- Light: one warm key, low, from the left at ~30°; no fill, no rim, long soft
  shadow to the right.
- Permitted difference between variants: **only** a small kraft roast tag on the
  tin tie ("LIGHT" / "DARK") and the loose beans, props and lighting warmth.
  Bag artwork is identical in every asset.

## The 7-point bag-consistency check (per asset)

1. Flat-bottom pouch silhouette with visible gussets and a double-folded roll-top.
2. Matte charcoal-kraft body, no gloss.
3. One unbroken ember band across the lower third.
4. "EMBER & OAK" legible, correctly spelled, one line, centred on the band.
5. One oak-leaf-and-flame mark above it, single line weight.
6. One round matte-black valve, upper right.
7. Copper tin-tie visible at the roll top.
